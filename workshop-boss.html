One platform. The whole repair journey.

End-to-end workshop management for the automotive body repair industry — from check-in to clearance.

1 · Intake & Quoting

1

Vehicle check-in

A digital check-in record for every arrival, condition photos attached and stored, signed PDF living on the job — no lost paperwork.

2

Check-in via camera

Walk-around capture from a phone, no clipboard — condition notes and signature captured on the spot, synced straight to the job file.

3

AI-powered quoting

Upload damage images and AI drafts the quote in the background — labour and paint rates apply automatically, ready to review in minutes.

4

Audatex integration

Pull an assessment by originator code and assessment number — vehicle, owner, insurance and claim details populate automatically.

5

Parts via Edge IQ

Send a job's parts list to suppliers and receive priced quotes back, with status and live totals rolling up on the job.

2 · Operations & People

6

Stock list & paint mixing

One central inventory with movement history, plus estimated vs. actual paint material cost tracked per job.

7

WhatsApp & SMS

Template-driven customer updates with merge fields, full conversation history stored on the job.

8

HR & attendance

Per-person working hours, custom schedules, attendance dashboards ready for biometric clock-in.

3 · Mobile & Handover

9

Mobile app + QR scanner

The full jobs board in your pocket, filterable by department and status — scan a job QR code to jump straight to that vehicle.

10

Clearance certificate

Generated on the phone at handover — name, email, betterment and signature captured, landing on the job instantly.
