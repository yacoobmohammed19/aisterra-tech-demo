Product demo · module walkthrough

One platform. The whole repair journey.

End-to-end workshop management for the automotive body repair industry — from check-in to clearance, four modules deep.

1 · Intake & Quoting

Vehicle check-in

Structured intake — a digital check-in record is generated for every vehicle arrival, with condition photos attached and stored, and the signed PDF living on the job. No lost paperwork, no arguments.

Check-in via camera

Walk-around capture from the phone camera during check-in — no clipboard required. Condition notes and the customer's signature are captured on the spot and sync straight to the job file.

AI-powered quoting

Upload damage images and AI damage assessment drafts the quote — processing in the background with notifications when ready. Labour and paint rates apply automatically, so the quote is ready for review in minutes, not hours.

Audatex integration

Direct import — pull an assessment using the originator code and assessment number. Vehicle, owner, insurance and claim details populate automatically, with pre-filled fields highlighted for review before submitting.

Parts via Edge IQ

Send a job's parts list to suppliers through Edge IQ and receive competitive priced quotes back. Status per line — allocated, invoiced, received, awaiting review — with live totals including VAT rolling up on the job.

Stock list & movements

One central inventory list across the workshop with part numbers and quantities. Recent stock movements are logged line by line, with invoice and demand quantities visible at a glance.

2 · Operations & People

Paint mixing & materials

Estimated paint material against actual mixed cost, with variance tracked per job. Each formula line carries volume, density and cost, and the retail mix price is calculated automatically from the formula.

WhatsApp & SMS

Template-driven updates like "Parts On Order" — workshop name, user and customer details merge into a live preview before sending. Full conversation history stored on the job, WhatsApp and SMS in one timeline.

HR & attendance

Manage workshop staff with per-person working hours, custom start/end/lunch schedules per weekday, plus attendance dashboards and daily summaries — ready for biometric clock-in devices.

3 · Mobile & Handover

Mobile app + QR scanner

The full jobs board in your pocket — filterable by department and status (assessed, quoted, booking, job cards, onsite and more). Scan a job QR code to jump straight to that vehicle, working in real time alongside desktop.

Clearance certificate — mobile

Generate the clearance certificate from the phone during handover. Customer name, email, betterment and signature captured digitally, and the certificate lands on the job the moment it's submitted.

Clearance documents

The signed clearance certificate sits in the job's documents tab alongside the check-in record — a complete, insurer-ready pack from arrival to handover, exportable as PDF.
