Why it works

One ecosystem, four connected platforms

Smart Quote drives the network. Workshop Boss manages the repair workflow. Moto Marketplace supports smarter parts sourcing. Aisterra Tech connects the ecosystem.

Can I use it standalone?

Yes — Smart Quote™ runs as a fast, standalone quoting tool while still connecting securely to the wider Aisterra ecosystem when you're ready.

Is it secure?

Yes. Built on secure Azure infrastructure with data protection designed for insurer, repairer and OEM-grade requirements.

Who is it for?

Repairers who need faster quote turnaround, insurers who need transparent claim quoting, and clients who want a clear, visual quote.

Where it sits in the workflow

Part of the estimate, not a separate step

Smart Quote is one of the connected estimating sources feeding Workshop Boss job records — alongside Audatex assessments and direct insurer PDF uploads — so a quote generated in Smart Quote lands straight on the job rather than being re-typed.

Part numbers & visuals

Quotes are built around real part numbers and supporting images, so what's quoted matches what's actually needed — not a rough estimate.

One shared record

Client, repairer and insurer are all looking at the same quote, the same revisions and the same detail — no version confusion over email.

Feeds the wider ecosystem

A Smart Quote can flow into a Workshop Boss job for repair workflow, or into Edge IQ for parts sourcing and comparison, without re-entry.
