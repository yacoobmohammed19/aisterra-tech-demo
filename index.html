Questions

What workshops usually ask first

Straight answers to the things owners and managers raise before they book a demo.

Do we have to replace the systems we already use?
No. Aisterra Tech is built around the systems workshops already run, from Audatex to your paint supplier. It connects them into one reliable workflow rather than forcing you to rip anything out.
How long until we are live?
Most workshops go live within 60 days. You begin in a test workspace, prove the value on one workflow, then expand once your team is comfortable.
Is our data secure?
Yes. Everything runs on secure Azure infrastructure with data protection designed for insurer, repairer and OEM grade requirements.
Can we start with just one platform?
Absolutely. Many workshops start with Workshop Boss or Smart Quote and add Moto Marketplace and Edge IQ later. One login already connects them when you are ready.
What does it cost to see it?
A demo and a pre-configured test workspace are free. You only commit once you have seen it working on your own jobs.
